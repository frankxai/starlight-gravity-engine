# The Gravity Manifesto

**Human engineering creates gravity. Agentic engineering makes it compound.**

---

Some people bend the space around them. Ideas find them. Collaborators show up.
Rooms form where they sit down. Opportunities arrive that no plan could have
scheduled. We call it luck, or charisma, or being well-connected — and then we
shrug, as if it were weather.

It is not weather. It is **gravity**, and gravity can be engineered.

Not manufactured. Not faked. Not automated into a spam cannon that DMs a
thousand strangers a templated compliment. Engineered the way a garden is
engineered — by tending real things until they compound.

## The five forces

Gravity is not one thing. It is the product of five:

$$\text{Gravity} = \text{Direction} \times \text{Signal} \times \text{Contribution} \times \text{Convening} \times \text{Reliability}$$

- **Direction** — You are moving somewhere, and it is legible. People can only
  join a vector, never a fog.
- **Signal** — You notice what you notice, and you keep it. Attention that
  evaporates compounds nothing.
- **Contribution** — You give before you ask. Generosity is the only durable
  form of reach.
- **Convening** — You bring people together around something true. A host
  creates a field a mere attendee never will.
- **Reliability** — Your promises come true. Trust is the interest rate on
  every relationship you have.

It is a **product**, not a sum. That is the whole point. A person with brilliant
signal and zero reliability has almost no gravity — the multiplication collapses.
You cannot buy your way out of a weak force with a strong one. You can only raise
the floor.

## What agents are for

Here is the temptation of this moment: to point the new machines at other people.
To scrape, to score, to rank, to auto-message, to simulate warmth at scale. That
road produces the opposite of gravity — it produces the uncanny, the resented,
the blocked.

Agents are not for manufacturing the forces. They are for **removing the friction
that keeps humans from expressing forces they already have.**

An agent can remember the name of the person you met eighteen months ago and the
thing they were struggling with. It can prepare you before the room so you arrive
generous instead of scrambling. It can turn the note you scrawled after dinner
into a publishable artifact by morning. It can notice that a promise is about to
go stale and hand you the chance to keep it. It can run the loop weekly so you
actually learn.

Memory. Consistency. Preparation. Transformation. Cycle frequency. Those are the
agentic contributions, and they are enormous.

But an agent does not manufacture **judgment, presence, generosity, trust,
relationship intent, or identity.** Those are yours. They are the only things
that were ever going to create the gravity in the first place.

## The line we do not cross

The human decides who to trust, what things mean, who gets introduced, what gets
said, and what gets published. Always. The agent never impersonates you, never
speaks as you without approval, never infers what someone secretly wants, never
ranks the people in your life, never turns a private conversation into public
content on its own.

We wrote those lines into the code, not just the README. Speculation is refused
at capture. A person record has no field for a score, because we made it
structurally impossible to add one. Nothing marked private can be published
without a human, a provenance chain, and an explicit act of approval.

## The invitation

Build a direction people can see. Keep the signals you notice. Give first.
Convene. Keep your word. Then let agents make each of those cheaper, faster, and
more consistent than you could sustain alone — so the field around you compounds
instead of leaking away.

The next personal operating system will not merely manage your tasks. It will
compound the ideas, relationships, rooms, and opportunities surrounding a human.

This is the engine for that.
